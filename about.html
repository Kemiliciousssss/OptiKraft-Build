About OptiKraft Technologies

OptiKraft Technologies was founded by two partners to supply flagship mirrorless cameras alongside genuine OEM internal replacement parts.

Our Kuala Lumpur optical cleanroom provides laser sensor alignment, mechanical shutter replacement, and lens fungus removal.
